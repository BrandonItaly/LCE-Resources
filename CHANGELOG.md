New textures have been added to cover content released since the Festive Mash-up’s Bedrock discontinuation, courtesy of the [Festive Mash-up Patch team](https://github.com/BrandonItaly/LCE-Resources/blob/festive/resources/CREDITS.md).

## Additions:

- Added support for 26.3:
    - Updated core shaders.
    - Updated trim color palette texture paths.

- Geyser and noxious gas particles

## Changes:

### Textures:
- Armor trim colors now match Festive materials
- Adjusted brown, orange, magenta, and lime harness colors
### Environment:
- Sulfur caves now have unique grass, foliage, and water colors (Polytone only)
### Entities:
- Zombie, Husk, and Drowned torso bottom textures are now facing the correct direction
- Drowned no longer have missing pixels on the front of the body
### Blocks:
- Activated Observer back texture no longer has flickering pixels

## Fixes:

- Fern (silver bell) painting is now centered
- Copper pickaxe no longer has a miscolored pixel
- Quartz and purpur pillar sides now support the 26.2 texture rename
